# Start here — LedgerLake

The full setup guide is the [README](../README.md) in this folder. Read that first. It covers install, clone, your team repo, and how to open a pull request.

## Your first task

Week 1 asks you to generate a CSV and upload it to MinIO. MinIO and Postgres are already in docker-compose.yml. Open MinIO at http://localhost:9001 with user `minio` and password `minio12345`.
